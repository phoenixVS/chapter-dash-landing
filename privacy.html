This Privacy Policy explains how Chapter Dash ("Chapter Dash," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve the App:

CategoryExamples
Account information Email address, display name, password (stored in hashed form), profile photo (optional)
Reading activity Reading speed, pages/chapters read, session duration, reading streaks and progress
Social & friend interactions Friends list, friend requests, activity shared with friends, comments, reactions, and reading challenges
Library data Books and titles added to your library, shelves/collections, ratings, and reviews you write
Subscription & purchase data Subscription tier and status, transaction identifiers processed by Apple App Store or Google Play (we do not receive or store your payment card details)
Device & usage data Device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, and general usage analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account and provide core App functionality
  • To track and display your reading activity, progress, and statistics
  • To enable social features such as friends, activity feeds, and shared challenges
  • To personalize recommendations and improve the App experience
  • To process and manage freemium subscriptions
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, or support requests
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual necessity — to provide the App and features you request, including your account and subscription
  • Consent — for optional features such as social sharing, marketing communications, and certain analytics
  • Legitimate interests — to maintain, secure, and improve the App, and to prevent fraud or misuse
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required to comply with applicable law

You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the App (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting, and customer support tooling), under contractual obligations to protect your data
  • App stores and payment processors (Apple App Store, Google Play) to process subscription purchases
  • Other users, limited to information you choose to make visible through social features (e.g., your display name, activity, and reviews shared with friends or made public)
  • Legal and regulatory authorities, where required to comply with law, protect rights, or respond to lawful requests
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Privacy Policy or a comparable one
Note: As of this policy's effective date, Chapter Dash does not use named third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs beyond standard app-store services. If/when such services (e.g., Firebase, RevenueCat) are integrated, this section and any required consent flows should be updated to name them specifically.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal, tax, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where we transfer personal data out of the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent legal mechanisms.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to certain processing of your data
  • Request a portable copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at chapterdashapp@gmail.com.

8. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell
  • Delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — Chapter Dash does not sell or share your personal information as defined under the CCPA
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of your CCPA rights

To submit a request, email chapterdashapp@gmail.com with the subject line "CCPA Request." We will verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

9. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account and associated personal data directly in the App:

  1. Open Chapter Dash and go to Profile → Settings
  2. Select Account
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Deleting your account removes your profile, library, reading activity, and social connections from active use within 30 days, subject to the retention exceptions described in Section 5. If you are unable to complete this in the App, email chapterdashapp@gmail.com from your account's registered email address and we will process the deletion manually.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

11. Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
